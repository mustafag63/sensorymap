**Project:** SensoryMap – AGÜ Sümer Campus

## 1. Introduction

This report defines six accessibility assessment criteria for evaluating campus buildings and nearby cafés. The criteria cover physical and sensory accessibility and are based on accessibility standards and inclusive design principles.

The aim is to collect consistent information about 12–15 locations and help users identify spaces suitable for their individual needs.

## 2. Physical Accessibility Criteria

### 2.1 Ramp Accessibility [1]

Ramps will be evaluated based on TS 9111 accessibility requirements.

- **Availability:** Available / Not available / Not required
- **Clear width:** Minimum 90 cm; 100 cm for public buildings according to the project proposal
- **Slope:** Measured in percentage (%), with project reference limits between 5% and 8% depending on height difference
- **Condition:** Accessible / Too steep / Obstructed

### 2.2 Elevator Accessibility [2]

Elevators will be assessed according to EN 81-70.

- **Availability:** Yes / No / Not required
- **Operational status:** Working / Not working
- **Cabin dimensions:** Reference size 110 × 140 cm for a Type 2 elevator
- **Clear door width:** Reference minimum 90 cm for Type 2 elevators
- **Wheelchair accessibility:** Accessible / Not accessible

### 2.3 Step-Free Entrance and Door Accessibility [1]

Each entrance will be assessed separately to identify accessible routes.

- **Entrance type:** Main / Secondary / Accessible alternative
- **Step-free access:** Yes / No
- **Threshold height:** Maximum 1.3 cm according to project criteria
- **Clear interior door width:** Minimum 90 cm according to project criteria
- **Door type:** Automatic / Manual
- **Opening difficulty:** Easy / Heavy
- **Opening hours:** Recorded for each entrance

The most accessible verified entrance will be identified as the recommended entrance.

## 3. Sensory Accessibility Criteria

### 3.1 Noise Level [3, 4]

Noise levels will be classified into three project-defined categories.

- **Quiet:** Below 50 dB
- **Moderate:** 50–70 dB
- **Loud:** Above 70 dB
- **Peak and off-peak conditions:** Recorded separately
- **Background music:** Present / Absent
- **Measurement details:** Date, time and device

Smartphone measurements will be presented as approximate noise ranges rather than exact sound levels.

### 3.2 Lighting Conditions [3]

Lighting conditions will be evaluated through observable environmental characteristics.

- **Natural daylight:** Available / Not available
- **Artificial lighting:** LED / Fluorescent / Other
- **Exposed fluorescent lighting:** Present / Absent
- **Naturally illuminated areas:** Available / Not available

### 3.3 Seating Density and Spatial Layout [3, 5]

Seating density and furniture arrangements will be evaluated to understand the spatial conditions of each location.

- **Seating density:** Sparse / Medium / Dense
- **Furniture arrangement:** Open / Closely arranged
- **Quiet corner or separate room:** Available / Not available
- **Alternative seating areas:** Available / Not available

The exact seating-density definitions will be finalized with Sena Nur Tuz in Week 2.

## 4. Additional Information and Data Verification

Each assessment will include the venue name, building, floor, entrance location, accessible toilet availability and last update date.

All recorded information will have one of three verification statuses:

- **Verified:** Confirmed by an on-site assessment or two independent user confirmations
- **Unverified:** Reported but not yet confirmed
- **No Data:** Information is unavailable

## 5. Assessment Method and Expected Outcome

Physical accessibility will be evaluated using measuring tapes, laser measures and digital incline gauges. Sensory characteristics will be documented through structured observation and noise measurements conducted with the electrical engineering team.

These criteria will be used to develop a standardized **Venue Assessment Form**, allowing each location to be assessed consistently in less than ten minutes.

The collected data will support the SensoryMap website and its accessibility filtering system.
